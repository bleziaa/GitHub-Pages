WHAT ARE ONLINE CLASSES?

Online classes are lessons delivered through the internet using digital platforms, allowing students to learn from different locations.

// MODULE 02 — POSITIVE REACTIONS

THE BENEFITS

Flexibility

Study with a more adaptable schedule.

Accessibility

Learn from almost anywhere.

Time-Saving

Reduce commuting time and costs.

Digital Skills

Improve technological abilities.

// MODULE 03 — ANOMALIES DETECTED

THE CHALLENGES

Internet Problems

Connection issues can interrupt lessons.

Distractions

Noise and social media can reduce concentration.

Limited Interaction

Online learning can reduce face-to-face communication.
